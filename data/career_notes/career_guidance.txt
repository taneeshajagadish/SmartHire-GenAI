SMART HIRE CAREER GUIDANCE

Resume Improvement:
A strong resume should clearly present relevant technical skills, education,
projects, internships, and work experience.

Software Engineer Skills:
Common skills include programming languages, data structures and algorithms,
databases, version control, problem solving, and software development practices.

Interview Preparation:
Candidates should practice technical questions, coding problems, project
explanations, and behavioral questions.

Project Presentation:
Candidates should be able to explain the problem, proposed solution,
technologies used, implementation, challenges, and results of their projects.

Job Matching:
A candidate should compare their skills and experience with the requirements
of a target job. Missing skills can be identified and prioritized for learning.

Career Growth:
Candidates can improve their employability by developing relevant technical
skills, building practical projects, gaining internship experience, and
continuously learning technologies relevant to their target role.
